Before You Ask

The Questions Everyone Asks

Why custom instead of Salesforce, Zoho, or SAP?

Those are built for everyone, which means they fit no one. You bend your process around the tool, pay per seat forever, and use a fraction of the features. Custom used to be the expensive option: hundreds of thousands and a year of consultants. AI cut that by an order of magnitude. Now you can own a system that fits exactly, for less than you'd spend renting one that doesn't.

How long does an ERP take to build?

Your first module (usually the CRM or the finance module, whichever bleeds most) is live in 2 to 4 weeks. A full ERP rolls out module by module, typically over 6 to 12 weeks. You'll know the timeline before we start. It's part of the architecture stage, in writing.

Do I need technical staff to run it?

No. That's the point. We build systems your existing team uses through interfaces they already know: WhatsApp, email, a dashboard. We handle the monitoring and maintenance. If your team can use a phone, they can use the system.

What if it doesn't work for my industry?

ERPs don't care about industries. They care about process. The playbook is the same everywhere, real estate to manufacturing: map the process, build the modules around it, connect the data. And if the audit shows a custom build genuinely isn't worth it for you, we'll tell you that in writing and you've lost 30 minutes, not a retainer.
