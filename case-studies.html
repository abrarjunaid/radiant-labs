Case 01 // CRM · Real Estate

Full-Stack Brokerage CRM with a Training Layer

Built For
A real estate brokerage in the UAE
The System
Leads flow in from every property portal and Meta ads, time-stamped and tagged to the exact property. The right agent gets a WhatsApp notification the second a lead lands, then manages it stage by stage through the conversion funnel, with AI drafting replies to clients along the way.
Beyond the Pipeline
A leaderboard shows every agent the deals their colleagues are closing. New agents complete a mandatory onboarding course, tests included, inside the same platform. Management sees the whole operation live.

Deal conversion up [X]%, and lead leakage from missed follow-ups down [X]%. Lead capture, follow-up, training, and performance running in one system.
