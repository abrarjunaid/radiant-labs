Your ERP, Module by Module

Every business needs the same three layers: a CRM that runs revenue, operations modules that run the back office, and dashboards that show you everything. We build all three around your process, not someone else's template. Start with the module that bleeds most.

Module 01

Bespoke CRM

What it is: A CRM built around your exact pipeline, not a template you bend to fit. Every stage, field, and rule matches how you actually sell, with AI agents on top answering inquiries in seconds, qualifying leads, booking appointments, and chasing every follow-up.

Who it's for: Teams paying per seat to bend a generic CRM around their process, or losing deals in chat threads and spreadsheets because the official tool doesn't fit.

  • CRM built around your exact pipeline
  • AI agents for instant response & follow-up
  • Qualification & appointment booking
  • Customer lifecycle & retention flows
Module 02

Operations & Finance Modules

What it is: The modules an off-the-shelf ERP charges a fortune for and still gets wrong: orders, projects, inventory, invoicing, reconciliation, statements. Built around your workflow and automated, so the back office runs on schedule without anyone touching it.

Who it's for: Businesses whose back office lives in spreadsheets, where month-end takes weeks and the same numbers get retyped into three different tools.

  • Orders, projects & inventory tracking
  • Invoicing & payment workflows
  • Reconciliation & statement generation
  • Data sync with your existing tools
Module 03

Reporting & Command Dashboards

What it is: The visibility layer on top of every module: one screen with every lead, order, and number that matters, updating in real time. One source of truth for the whole company, with a full audit trail of who did what and when.

Who it's for: Operators running on gut feel and month-old reports. If sales, operations, and finance each have their own version of the numbers, this is you.

  • Real-time KPI dashboards
  • Cross-department reporting
  • Full audit trails
  • Alerts when numbers drift
Across Every Module

The AI Layer

What it is: We don't bolt a chatbot onto your ERP. We AI-enable the platform itself, threading intelligence through every module: agents working your pipeline inside the CRM, AI-drafted replies and follow-ups, document and data extraction feeding the back office, and anomaly alerts the moment a reporting number drifts.

Who it's for: Every build. The AI layer is a big part of why custom now beats off-the-shelf: your ERP doesn't just record the operation, it runs parts of it, and gets more capable every month.

  • AI agents inside the CRM
  • AI-drafted comms & follow-ups
  • Document & data extraction
  • Anomaly detection & alerts
