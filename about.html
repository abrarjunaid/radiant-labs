About

We Build Systems Because We Run Businesses.

Radiant Labs is the AI systems division of Radiant Group, a Dubai-based operating group spanning real estate brokerage, short-term rental and property management, and advisory.

We didn't start as an agency selling AI. We started as operators drowning in software that didn't fit: leads scattered across WhatsApp threads, owner statements rebuilt by hand every month, reports assembled from five different tools at midnight. So we built our own ERP, module by module: a proprietary CRM, finance and reconciliation modules, reporting engines, lead pipelines.

Those systems didn't just save time. They changed what the businesses could do. Radiant Labs exists to build that same advantage for other businesses. AI changed the economics: bespoke systems that once cost hundreds of thousands and took a year now ship in weeks. And the same playbook fits any industry, from real estate and hospitality to marketing and manufacturing.

That order of operations matters. Every module we sell has been battle-tested inside Radiant Group's own operations first. That means when we tell you something works, it's because we're running it, not because it demoed well.

Operator-First

We eat our own cooking

Every system we sell started as a fix for a problem in one of our own companies. If it doesn't survive real operations, it doesn't get sold.

Skin in the Game
